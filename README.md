# SkillCraft-AI

> **Learn → Build → Prove → Earn**

A platform for students, beginners, and career switchers who want practical, job-relevant skills — and a way to turn those skills into real paid opportunities.

## What Is SkillCraft-AI?

SkillCraft-AI is built around one idea: the gap between "I want to learn a skill" and "I got paid to use that skill" should not be as wide as it currently is.

Most learning platforms give you content. SkillCraft-AI gives you a path — from knowing nothing to having a portfolio to getting paid by a real client.

---

## The Core Loop

```
LEARN
  ↓  Pick a career goal (Web Dev, Data Science, UI/UX, Marketing, etc.)
  ↓  AI generates a personalized roadmap
  ↓  Structured resources, AI guidance on using AI tools while learning

BUILD
  ↓  Work through real-world projects tied to your roadmap
  ↓  Guided, practical, portfolio-worthy work

PROVE
  ↓  Your work becomes a public portfolio
  ↓  Not just a certificate — actual proof of what you can do

EARN
  ↓  Real clients discover your portfolio projects
  ↓  Client approaches you, project is scoped
  ↓  You do the work, you get paid
```

Then you level up, pick a harder goal, and the loop repeats.

---

## Why Does This Exist?

The founder identified four pain points that existing platforms don't solve together:

1. **"I don't know what to learn"** — Too much content online, none of it mapped to real jobs
2. **"I don't know how to use AI while learning"** — AI is reshaping every field, but nobody teaches learners to integrate it into their workflow
3. **"I can't prove I know it"** — Certificates don't convince clients or employers; a portfolio of real work does
4. **"I can't find my first client"** — The gap from "I learned this" to "someone paid me" is the hardest part

SkillCraft-AI is one platform that addresses all four.

## How the 600-Day Build Works

SkillCraft-AI is being built over 600+ days, phase by phase:

| Phase | Days | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Foundation | 1–30 | Planning, architecture, auth skeleton |
| 2 — Learn Flow | 31–90 | AI roadmap generation, domain selection |
| 3 — Build & Portfolio | 91–180 | Projects, submissions, public portfolio pages |
| 4 — Client Marketplace | 181–300 | Clients discover and approach learners |
| 5 — Payments | 301–450 | Money moves from client to learner |
| 6 — Scale & Polish | 451–600+ | More domains, AI tutor, growth |

Each phase delivers something a real user can actually use before the next phase begins.

This is not a sprint. Meaningful platforms take time to build well. This roadmap doesn't pretend otherwise.
